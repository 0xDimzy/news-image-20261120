ASIA · ODISHA, INDIA

Akvan comes ashore; a state moves indoors

The cyclone crossed near Gopalpur between 07:30 and 08:30 IST, with winds of 70–80 km/h gusting to 90. Officials counted more than 300,000 people in 9,341 shelters; no deaths reported.

Reported by IMD and PTI.
EUROPE · KYIV, UKRAINE

One night, three bridges hit

An overnight assault of roughly 400 drones and more than 100 missiles damaged all three Dnipro crossings. Mayor Klitschko put Kyiv's toll at 3 dead and 15 hurt, with 8 killed nationwide.

Reported by Reuters; toll via Mayor Klitschko.
GLOBAL · COP31, ANTALYA

Climate talks reach the last morning empty-handed

The closing day opened without an agreement. A fresh draft had dropped fossil-fuel wording and the $120bn adaptation figure; hosts pushed “35 by 35” and Panama warned it could block.

Reported from the summit floor.
