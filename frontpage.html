1 ASIA · WEATHER

Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in Odisha

The weakened cyclonic storm crossed the north Andhra Pradesh–south Odisha coast near Gopalpur on Friday morning with winds of 70–80 km/h gusting to 90, the IMD said. More than 300,000 people were evacuated and 423 rescue teams deployed; officials reported no major damage or loss of life. 209 mm of rain fell at Patrapur; red alerts covered six Andhra districts.

2 EUROPE · WAR

Russian barrage hits three Kyiv bridges

A ballistic-missile attack damaged the Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi bridges over the Dnipro — the biggest strikes on Kyiv's crossings since the war began. Three people were killed and 15 injured, Mayor Klitschko said; emergency power cuts spread across the capital.

3 ASIA · MARKETS

AI rout deepens across Asia

Japan's Nikkei fell 4.8%, South Korea's Kospi 5.7% and Taiwan's Taiex 6.75% — on track for its worst week since 1990 — as doubts over AI valuations spread. Gold slid below $3,600 to its lowest in over a year.
